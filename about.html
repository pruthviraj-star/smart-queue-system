About SmartQueue

SmartQueue is a virtual queue management system built for GH Raisoni College of Engineering and Management, Jalgaon. It eliminates physical waiting lines by letting students claim virtual tokens, track their position in real-time, and get notified when their turn arrives.

Why SmartQueue?

Every semester, students spend hours standing in queues at the Fees Counter, Admissions Office, and Scholarship Section. There's no way to know how long the wait will be or how many people are ahead. SmartQueue solves this — students can join a queue from anywhere on campus, monitor their position live, and only head to the counter when their token is called.

What it does

Virtual Token System

Students get a unique sequential token number when they join a queue — no physical slips needed.

Real-time Position Tracking

Queue position updates instantly as staff calls the next token — powered by Firebase Firestore live sync.

Push Notifications

Students receive browser push notifications when they are 3 positions away and when it's their turn — even if the app is in the background.

Staff Dashboard

Staff can call next token, mark service complete, open/close the queue, and reset it at end of day.

Admin Analytics

Admin dashboard shows today's token stats, students served, average service time per department, and queue status overview.

AI Wait Time Prediction

Estimated wait time is calculated using real service history data from completed tokens — not a static guess.
